Welcome

I am a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at The University of Akron. My research focuses on active matter, magnetic microswimmers, self-phoretic particles, and low-Reynolds-number fluid dynamics.

I use computational modeling, high-performance computing, and theoretical analysis to investigate collective behavior, pattern formation, phase transitions, and the dynamics of microswimmers near boundaries.

Research Interests

  • Active matter and collective behavior
  • Magnetic and polar microswimmers
  • Self-phoresis of Janus particles
  • Low-Reynolds-number hydrodynamics
  • Boundary element methods
  • Particle-based simulations and high-performance computing
